# NMR Pilot Project: Starting References

Take a look and pick a starting point that looks interesting. We did pKa determination a few years ago. Or search NMR / J Chem Ed on SciFinder, etc.

## Equilibrium: pKa and self-association

- Gift, A. D.; Stewart, S. M.; Kwete Bokashanga, P. Experimental
  Determination of pKa Values by Use of NMR Chemical Shifts, Revisited.
  *J. Chem. Educ.* **2012**, 89, 1458–1460. [doi:10.1021/ed200433z](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed200433z)
  (substituted pyridines in D2O; a substituent series gives a Hammett
  plot as the organic tie-in)
- Handloser, C. S.; Chakrabarty, M. R.; Mosher, M. W. Experimental
  Determination of pKa Values by Use of NMR Chemical Shift. *J. Chem.
  Educ.* **1973**, 50, 510. [doi:10.1021/ed050p510](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed050p510)
- Zivkovic, A. et al. Low Field NMR Determination of pKa Values for
  Hydrophilic Drugs for Students in Medicinal Chemistry.
  *Magnetochemistry* **2017**, 3, 29. [doi:10.3390/magnetochemistry3030029](https://doi.org/10.3390/magnetochemistry3030029)
  (benchtop NMR, so the closest match to our instrument)
- Baral, R.; Gunn, J.; Hartley, C. S. Quantifying the Association of
  Caffeine Using NMR Spectroscopy in the Chemistry Laboratory. *J. Chem.
  Educ.* **2025**, 102, 4611–4615. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.5c00613](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.5c00613)
  (dilution shifts in D2O, isodesmic model, K ≈ 10 M⁻¹)

## Keto–enol tautomerism

- Cortney, C. H.; Krishnan, V. V. Keto–Enol Tautomerization of
  Acetylacetone in Mixed Solvents by NMR Spectroscopy (Onsager–Kirkwood
  solvation). *J. Chem. Educ.* **2020**, 97, 825–830.
  [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00737](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00737)
- Sandusky, P. Expansion of the Classic Acetylacetone Physical Chemistry
  Laboratory NMR Experiment: Correlation of the Enol–Keto Equilibrium
  Position with the Solvent Dipole Moment. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2014**,
  91, 739–742. [doi:10.1021/ed400583q](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed400583q)
- Atkinson, D.; Chechik, V. An NMR Study of Isotope Effect on Keto-Enol
  Tautomerization. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2004**, 81, 1030.
  [doi:10.1021/ed081p1030](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed081p1030) (H/D exchange of butanone with kinetic isotope
  effects; the most physical-organic option)

## Host–guest / supramolecular

- Tardajos, G.; González-Gaitano, G. Chemical Equilibrium in
  Supramolecular Systems as Studied by NMR Spectrometry. *J. Chem. Educ.*
  **2004**, 81, 270. [doi:10.1021/ed081p270](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed081p270) (alkanoates in cyclodextrin)
- Young, M. C. et al. Resorcin[4]arenes: A Convenient Scaffold To Study
  Supramolecular Self-Assembly and Host:Guest Interactions. *J. Chem.
  Educ.* **2019**, 96, 781–785. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00722](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00722)
- Go, E. B.; Srisuknimit, V.; Cheng, S. L.; Vosburg, D. A.
  Self-Assembly, Guest Capture, and NMR Spectroscopy of a Metal–Organic
  Cage in Water. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2016**, 93, 368–371.
  [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.5b00714](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.5b00714)

## Kinetics / reaction monitoring

- Welch, B. G.; Cantillo, D. Merging Inline NMR Monitoring and Flow
  Chemistry in the Undergraduate Organic Chemistry Laboratory. *J. Chem.
  Educ.* **2024**, 101, 3459–3465. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.4c00429](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.4c00429)

## Low-field spectra and spin simulation

- Edgar, M.; Percival, B. C.; Gibson, M.; Masania, J.; Beresford, K.;
  Wilson, P. B.; Grootveld, M. Benchtop NMR Spectroscopy and Spectral
  Analysis of the cis- and trans-Stilbene Products of the Wittig
  Reaction. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2019**, 96, 1938–1947.
  [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00657](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00657) (strong coupling at 40–80 MHz,
  simulated)

## Relaxation

- Kent, J. E.; Bell, N. G. A. Molecular Properties of Caffeine Explored
  by NMR: A Benchtop NMR Experiment for Undergraduate Physical-Chemistry
  Laboratories. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2019**, 96, 786–791.
  [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00633](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00633) (T1/T2; pairs well with the Baral
  caffeine association paper)
- Římal, V.; Bunyatova, E. I.; Štěpánková, H. Efficient Scavenging of
  TEMPOL Radical by Ascorbic Acid in Solution and Related Prolongation
  of 13C and 1H Nuclear Spin Relaxation Times of the Solute. *Molecules*
  **2024**, 29, 738. [doi:10.3390/molecules29030738](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules29030738) (radical scavenging
  read out through T1. The radical shortens T1 in proportion to its
  concentration, 1/T1 = 1/T1° + r1[TEMPOL], and an antioxidant that
  reduces it to the diamagnetic hydroxylamine lets T1 recover.)
