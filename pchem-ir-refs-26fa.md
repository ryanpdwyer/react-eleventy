# IR Pilot Project: Starting References

Take a look and pick a starting point that looks interesting. We have the new Agilent Spectrum Two FT-IR with a diamond ATR crystal, so liquids and solids go straight on the crystal. Or search IR / J Chem Ed on SciFinder, etc.

## Process analytical: following a reaction with IR

The idea is to track reactant and product bands over time and fit a kinetic model. With our top-plate ATR, you can either put a drop of the reaction mixture on the crystal (cover it so it doesn't evaporate) or spot aliquots onto it every minute or so.

- Haji, S.; Erkey, C. Kinetics of Hydrolysis of Acetic Anhydride by In-Situ FTIR Spectroscopy: An Experiment for the Undergraduate Laboratory. *Chem. Eng. Educ.* **2005**, 39, 56–61. [Link](https://journals.flvc.org/cee/article/view/122621) (Blake did this reaction by NMR, so you could compare the two techniques)
- Chen, H.; Yang, H. Online ATR-FT-IR for Real-Time Monitoring of the Aspirin Esterification Process in an Acid-Mediated Reaction. *Spectroscopy* **2022**, 37 (S8), 7–12. [doi:10.56530/spectroscopy.sp4582i4](https://doi.org/10.56530/spectroscopy.sp4582i4) (uses PCA to pull out component time traces)
- Fedick, P. W.; Schrader, R. L.; Ayrton, S. T.; Pulliam, C. J.; Cooks, R. G. Process Analytical Technology for Online Monitoring of Organic Reactions by Mass Spectrometry and UV–Vis Spectroscopy. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2019**, 96, 124–131. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00725](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00725) (good background on what PAT is)

Chem. Educ.* **1993**, 70, A3. [doi:10.1021/ed070pA3](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed070pA3)

## Gas-phase IR (needs a gas cell)

- Feller, S.; Blaich, C. Error Estimates for Fitted Parameters: Application to HCl/DCl Vibrational-Rotational Spectroscopy. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2001**, 78, 409. [doi:10.1021/ed078p409](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed078p409) (rotational constants, anharmonicity, and isotope effects, with a careful look at fit uncertainties)
- Bigham, N.; Denchy, M.; Kegerreis, J. A Simplified Technique for the Collection of an HCl/DCl Gas Mixture. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2018**, 95, 2298–2300. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00702](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00702)

## Path length, ATR, and quantitation

A transmission cell with variable path length would let you test Beer–Lambert directly in the IR and compare it with ATR, where the effective path length (the penetration depth) grows with wavelength.

- Stewart, S.; Sommer, A. Variable Path-Length Cells for Discovery-Based Investigation of the Beer–Lambert Law. *J. Chem. Educ.* **1999**, 76, 399. [doi:10.1021/ed076p399](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed076p399) (done in the visible, but the same idea)
- Schuttlefield, J. D.; Grassian, V. H. ATR–FTIR Spectroscopy in the Undergraduate Chemistry Laboratory. Part I: Fundamentals and Examples. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2008**, 85, 279. [doi:10.1021/ed085p279](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed085p279)
- Grassian, V. H.; Schuttlefield, J. D.; Larsen, S. C. ATR–FTIR Spectroscopy in the Undergraduate Chemistry Laboratory. Part II: A Physical Chemistry Laboratory Experiment on Surface Adsorption. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2008**, 85, 282. [doi:10.1021/ed085p282](https://doi.org/10.1021/ed085p282)
- Accettone, S. L. W.; DeFrancesco, C.; Van Belle, L.; Smith, J.; Giroux, E. A Problem-Based Approach to Teaching the Internal Standard Method by ATR-FTIR Spectroscopy. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2022**, 99, 3735–3746. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.2c00548](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.2c00548)
- Zapata, F.; López-Fernández, A.; Ortega-Ojeda, F.; Quintanilla, G.; García-Ruiz, C.; Montalvo, G. Introducing ATR-FTIR Spectroscopy through Analysis of Acetaminophen Drugs. *J. Chem. Educ.* **2021**, 98, 2675–2686. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.0c01231](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.0c01231)
