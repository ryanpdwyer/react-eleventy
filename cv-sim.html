Voltammetry Simulation

Redox = electron transfer.

OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons).
LEO the lion says GER: Losing Electrons is Oxidation, Gaining Electrons is Reduction.

Here, O + e− ⇌ R: O is the oxidized form (hollow: missing the electron), R the reduced form (filled).

A negative electrode pushes electrons into O (reduction); a positive one pulls them out of R (oxidation). This simulation plots reduction current as positive.

Only the working electrode is shown. The circuit is completed at a counter electrode, which does the opposite reaction. In water with an inert electrolyte that is the solvent itself: 2H2O → O2 + 4H+ + 4e− while O is reduced here, and 2H2O + 2e− → H2 + 2OH− while R is oxidized.

□O2 + 1e− ⇌ R2
